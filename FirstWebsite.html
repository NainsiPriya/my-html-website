Explanation Capability approach

The capability approach is a physical theoretical framework. This is italic text.It emphasizes the importance of enabling people to lead lives they value, rather than solely focusing on economic wealth or resources. This approach considers various factors such as education, health, social inclusion, and personal development, recognizing that these elements contribute to an individual's overall quality of life. By prioritizing capabilities, policymakers and researchers can better understand and address inequalities, ultimately promoting a more just and equitable society.
